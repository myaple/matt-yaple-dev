Pre-Flight Briefing

non-resume edition

I started as an aerospace engineer — Virginia Tech, BSc 2014 — and somewhere between spacecraft simulations and streaming telemetry I found the thing I actually love: systems that have to be right, fast, and always on.

Since then: intelligence-community-adjacent data platforms, a hundred-node processing cluster, a suspicious number of Kafka topics, and a genuine soft spot for Rust — the language that lets you move fast and sleep at night.

Lately I obsess over fully local LLM inference — running real models on real hardware inside locked-down environments — and teaching the engineers around me to do the same.

Off the clock: a Ducati Multistrada V2, melodic and power metal (Brothers of Metal on repeat), a homelab that refuses to stay small, and two kids who are convinced Daddy builds “computer things”.
